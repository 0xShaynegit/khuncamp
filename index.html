Common questions

What do I actually have to do?

Film when we give you the shot list. Show up for the monthly strategy call. That is it. Everything else is handled.

What is the difference between the build fee and the retainer?

The $3,000 build is a one-time investment. We learn your brand, build your creative system, set up tracking, and produce the first round of content. The $1,500/mo retainer is everything after that: ongoing content, posting, replies, and the monthly report. The build is the foundation. The retainer runs it.

What platforms do you post on?

We map that out on the first call based on where your audience is. Most clients run YouTube, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. We adapt to what fits your offer and the people you are trying to reach.

How long before content drives actual customers?

Typically 60 to 90 days before content drives consistent inbound. The first month is building the system right so every month after compounds instead of starting over. If you are looking for a spike, this is not the right fit and we will tell you that on the call.

Is there a minimum commitment?

We ask for three months minimum on the retainer. Content growth is a compounding channel. Three months is enough to see real data on what is working.

What if it is not working?

That is what the monthly report is for. If something is not converting, we see it in the data and we adjust. You will not hear "keep posting and trust the process" without a reason.

Ready to hand off the channel?

Book a call. We will look at your content, your offer, and your audience, and tell you exactly how we would run it.
