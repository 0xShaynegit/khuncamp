How It Works

Lead clicks a link.
Appointment lands in your calendar.

The booking system handles everything from the moment a lead is ready to schedule - no human touchpoints required until the appointment itself.

01

Lead receives a booking link

Via text, email, your website, or directly from the AI Receptionist - the lead gets a link to your live calendar with your available slots in real time.

02

They pick a time and confirm

The booking widget shows only your open slots. They pick one, fill in their details, and confirm - no login required, works on mobile.

03

Instant confirmation sent to both parties

The lead gets a confirmation text and email with all the details. Your calendar updates automatically. No manual entry, no double-bookings.

04

Automated reminders fire before the appointment

The system sends reminder texts 24 hours and 1 hour before the appointment. No-show rates drop dramatically.

05

Post-appointment follow-up triggered automatically

After the appointment, automated follow-up messages go out - review requests, rebooking prompts, or whatever fits your business model.
