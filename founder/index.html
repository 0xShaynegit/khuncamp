The story

From artillery fire
control to content
operating systems

I joined the US Army because my mother dared me to. Served 2017–2020, nine months in Korea, honorably discharged as an E-4 Specialist. My job was artillery fire control — someone runs the numbers before anything moves, and the numbers have to be right the first time.

After discharge, I joined Gamer's Little Playground — a 3 million-subscriber YouTube channel. Spent three years learning what scale actually looks like inside a content operation. Earned both Gold and Silver YouTube Play Buttons along the way.

Then I left to build my own channel from scratch. Hit 60,000 subscribers. In February 2025, I started Khun Camp to package that system for other creators and businesses.
