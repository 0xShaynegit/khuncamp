I joined the US Army because my mother dared me to.

I was doing a science project with a friend, said it to get a rise out of her, and she gave me a look that meant you couldn't if you tried. I went to the recruiter the next day. Picked the Army over the Air Force because the Army recruiter showed up in combat boots and the Air Force guy showed up in dress blues. Picked 13J, fire control, mostly because I thought I was picking 13B.

That is the honest version. My whole reasoning was pride and a uniform.

I served from 2017 to February 2020 and was honorably discharged. Nine months of it in Korea. Most of my actual days were spent in a motor pool doing maintenance and waiting. The job itself was artillery fire control. Somebody runs the numbers before anything moves and the numbers have to be right the first time. I got out an E-4, specialist, junior enough that the numbers were rarely mine. I just watched how much rode on them.

I separated in February 2020, a few weeks before the world shut down. I planned to go to school. Then COVID closed the campuses. I do not learn well online. So I went to work at a friend's company instead, making money playing video games, and started building a channel on the side. That turned into 60,000 subscribers and, at the peak, nine videos a day.

That taught me the thing most operators never get to learn: what actually makes a stranger stop, trust you, and act.

Now I run a content growth company out of Southeast Asia.

What I actually do

Most creators and business owners know they need to be putting out content. What they do not have is the system to do it without it consuming their week.

Khun Camp handles the whole thing. Strategy, scripts, filming direction, editing, posting, and community replies. You film when we ask. We handle everything else.

The goal is content that actually converts, not just content that exists.

If the math does not work for your situation, we say that on the call.

Where I work from

Khun Camp was founded in Da Nang, Vietnam. I do not live there.

I rotate through Southeast Asia. Thailand, Vietnam, the Philippines right now, more countries coming. I have been traveling since I was two months old. This is not a lifestyle I picked up as an adult. It is just the shape my life has always had.
