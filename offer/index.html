Common questions

What do I actually have to do?

Film the content when we give you the shot list. Show up for the monthly strategy call. That is it. Everything else is handled.

What platforms do you post on?

We map that out on the first call based on where your audience is. Most clients run YouTube, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. We adapt to what fits your offer and your ICP.

How long before I see results?

Typically 60 to 90 days before content drives consistent inbound. The first month is building the system right so every month after compounds, not resets.

Is there a minimum commitment?

We ask for three months minimum. Content growth is a compounding channel. If you come in looking for a spike, this is not the right fit and we will tell you that on the call.

What if the content is not working?

That is what the monthly report is for. If something is not converting, we see it in the data and we adjust. You will never hear "keep posting and trust the process" without a reason.
