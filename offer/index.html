Common questions

Do I need to already have a CRM or existing software?

No. We set everything up from scratch using GHL (GoHighLevel). If you already have one, we can work with it. You do not need to come in with anything pre-built.

What does the AI voice agent actually do?

It answers live inbound calls when you or your team cannot. It qualifies the lead using your intake questions, takes a message, and triggers follow-up sequences with no human required. It sounds like a professional receptionist, not a robot.

How fast does content convert to actual customers?

Typically 60 to 90 days to see consistent inbound from content. The AI Systems track produces results faster, often in the first week, because it works on calls that are already coming in.

Can I combine both tracks?

Yes. Several clients run both. The AI handles inbound that content generates, so the two compound. Ask about bundled pricing on the call.

What if it is not working?

During the warranty period we revise until it does. After that, we do a paid audit to find the gap. We do not disappear after the invoice clears.

Not sure which track fits?

Book a call. We will look at your numbers, tell you which track makes sense, and give you a straight answer. Even if it is not one of these.
